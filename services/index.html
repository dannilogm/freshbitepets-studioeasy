How a visit works

From your driveway to a fresher smile

Every visit happens at your home, in our mobile unit or wherever your pet feels most at ease. Most pets are done in under an hour.

  1. 01

    Meet and settle in

    We say hello, let your pet sniff around and take a few minutes to build trust. Nothing starts until your pet is relaxed.

  2. 02

    Quick oral check

    We look at the teeth and gums and ask about diet, chewing habits and your pet's history, so we know what to focus on.

  3. 03

    Gentle plaque and tartar care

    Using natural, non-invasive techniques, we manage visible plaque and tartar on the tooth surfaces. No anesthesia and no sedatives.

  4. 04

    Finishing and fresh breath

    We polish and finish with pet-safe products for a cleaner feel and fresher breath.

  5. 05

    Photos and your report

    You get before and after photos, notes on anything we noticed and a simple home routine to keep results longer.

Is preventive oral care right for your pet?

A good fit for

  • Healthy dogs and cats with mild to moderate plaque or tartar
  • Pets that are nervous at clinics and do better at home
  • Senior pets whose owners want to limit anesthesia between vet cleanings
  • Pets on a regular maintenance routine recommended by their vet

See your veterinarian first if

  • Your pet has a broken or loose tooth, swelling or bleeding gums
  • Your pet is in pain, drooling a lot or has stopped eating
  • There is heavy tartar below the gumline or it's been years without dental care
  • Your pet has a medical condition your vet is actively treating

Our care is maintenance between veterinary visits. It does not diagnose, treat disease or replace a professional dental cleaning under anesthesia when your vet recommends one.

Why plans follow your pet's size

A 12-pound Chihuahua and a 140-pound Great Dane need very different amounts of time, handling and product. Grouping plans by weight lets us plan each visit around your pet: smaller pets get short, calm sessions with more breaks, while large and giant breeds get extra time and a second pair of hands when needed. Cats have their own plan because they are handled differently from dogs, at any size.

How often should you book? For most pets we suggest a visit every six months, alongside your vet's yearly exam. Pets that build tartar quickly, such as many small breeds and seniors, may do better every three to four months. We will tell you honestly what we see at the end of each visit.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how our visits work, who they're for and how they fit with your vet's care.

Is anesthesia-free oral care safe?

Yes, when it is done by a trained technician on a pet that is calm and comfortable. We never force a pet. If your pet is too stressed, we stop and suggest another approach.

Does this replace a dental cleaning at the vet?

No. We work on visible tooth surfaces as preventive maintenance. Only a veterinarian can examine below the gumline, take X-rays and treat dental disease. Our visits help keep things in good shape between those appointments.

How long does a visit take?

Usually 30 to 60 minutes per pet, depending on size, temperament and how much buildup there is.

What if my pet is anxious or has never had a cleaning?

That is common. We go slowly, use breaks and gentle handling, and many of our clients started with pets that were scared of the vet.

Do you work with cats?

Yes. Cats have their own plan, with handling adapted to how cats like to be held.

Which areas do you serve?

We are mobile across Greater Dallas-Fort Worth, including Celina, Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Plano, Irving, Frisco, McKinney and Denton. Ask us if your city is not listed.

Can you see more than one pet in the same visit?

Yes. Households with several pets can book them together, and we have family plans for that.
