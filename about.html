The Founder

Meet Daiana de Paula

FreshBite Pets emerged from the passion of our founder, a pet care specialist who was looking for a safe, natural way to support pets' oral health between veterinary visits.

Full founder story

Combining years of experience and technical knowledge, Daiana founded FreshBite Pets with a clear goal: offering professional preventive oral maintenance that works in harmony with veterinary care and never takes its place. Her passion shows in every interaction with clients and in every healthy smile the team helps maintain.

At FreshBite Pets, oral maintenance is a way to promote a better quality of life and a stronger bond between owners and their pets, through consistent, responsible prevention.

Our mission

Healthy smiles between vet visits.
