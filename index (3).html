Privacy policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains how FreshBite Pets Natural Oral Care LLC ("FreshBite Pets", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website, book a visit, contact us or receive text messages from us. We provide mobile preventive oral care for dogs and cats in the Greater Dallas-Fort Worth area, Texas.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, service address, city and zip code, your pet's name, species, breed, weight, age and vaccination status, your contact preferences and any message you send.
  • Booking information: when you book online, the details you enter are collected through our scheduling provider (see "Services we use" below).
  • Visit records: notes and before-and-after photos of your pet's teeth taken during a visit.
  • Basic technical data: like any website, our hosting provider may log your IP address, browser type and pages visited to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

  • To schedule, confirm and carry out your pet's visits.
  • To reach you about appointments, reminders, follow-ups and questions you send us.
  • To keep records of your pet's care and share visit notes and photos with you.
  • To improve our service and keep this website secure.

We may show before-and-after photos of pets' teeth on our website and social media. These photos do not identify you. If you prefer that your pet's photos are not used, tell us and we will not use them.

Text messages (SMS)

If you check the SMS consent box or give us your number to receive texts, we may send you conversational messages about your appointments and questions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of booking.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Text messaging opt-in data is never shared with anyone except the service providers that deliver the messages for us.

When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it:

  • With service providers that help us run the business (scheduling, messaging, email and website hosting), only as needed to provide their service to us.
  • With your veterinarian, if you ask us to.
  • When required by law or to protect the safety of people or pets.

Services we use

  • MoeGo for online booking and appointment management. Information entered in the booking calendar is handled under MoeGo's own privacy policy.
  • YouTube for the video on our site. The video only loads if you press play, and YouTube may then set its own cookies.
  • Our website hosting provider, which keeps standard server logs.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies of its own.

How long we keep it

We keep client and pet records for as long as you use our services and for a reasonable time afterward for record-keeping, or as required by law. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information we have about you.
  • Opt out of text messages by replying STOP, or of emails by asking us.
  • Ask us not to use your pet's photos.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including using secure (HTTPS) connections and trusted service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Children

Our services are for adult pet owners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
