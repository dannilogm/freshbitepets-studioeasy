Our story

It all started with a little Chihuahua named Poli.

Hi, I'm Daiana de Paula, founder of FreshBite Pets. This is how my love for one small, anxious dog became the way we care for yours.

01 · A lifelong love

Animals have always been part of my life

I grew up surrounded by animals, and I always dreamed of turning that love into something that could make a real difference.

When I moved to the United States, I had the chance to work for a company specialized in natural dental cleaning for pets. That is where I discovered a whole world of possibilities and started to see a bigger purpose.

02 · Poli

My little Poli turned that interest into a mission

Poli isn't just my dog. She is my companion, my family, a piece of my heart.

Back in Brazil, Poli went through a procedure under anesthesia and almost lost her life. That is when we found out she has a heart murmur, and that any future anesthesia would need special care. Like anyone who deeply loves their pet, I was scared, and I needed to find ways to care for her and help prevent oral problems.

There was another challenge: Poli is a very anxious, fearful and sensitive Chihuahua. I knew that caring for her took more than a technique. I had to earn her trust, respect her time and understand her limits.

03 · The FreshBite Method

I didn't want just a cleaning

Thinking of Poli, I began studying natural, preventive oral care in depth. I wanted an experience where she felt safe, respected and loved.

From that inspiration I developed my own approach, combining preventive oral hygiene with music therapy, aromatherapy, moments of play and affection, and even fun photos to make every visit feel welcoming.

That is how the FreshBite Method was born, built on the belief that no two pets are alike. Each one has its own personality, fears, history and pace.

Today

The FreshBite Method, at your door

Today, FreshBite Pets brings this personalized care to other families' homes, offering preventive oral hygiene without anesthesia and always respecting each pet's needs and limits.

What started with my love for Poli became my mission: to care for other pets with the same patience, dedication and affection I always wanted for her. Because for us, it was never just about clean teeth. It's about trust, prevention and love.

Prevention is love in action.
