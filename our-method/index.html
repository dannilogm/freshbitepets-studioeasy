See it in action

Prevention that fits your pet's routine

Plaque builds up on teeth every day and, over time, hardens into tartar. Regular preventive visits remove visible buildup on the tooth surfaces above the gumline, and the home care tips in your smile report help you keep that routine going between your vet's dental exams.

Is preventive oral care right for your pet?

A good fit for

  • Healthy dogs and cats of all breeds and sizes with mild to moderate plaque or tartar
  • Pets that are nervous at clinics and do better at home
  • Senior pets whose owners want to limit anesthesia between vet cleanings
  • Pets on a regular maintenance routine recommended by their vet

See your veterinarian first if

  • Your pet has a broken or loose tooth, swelling or bleeding gums
  • Your pet is in pain, drooling a lot or has stopped eating
  • There is heavy tartar below the gumline or it's been years without dental care
  • Your pet has a medical condition your vet is actively treating

What our care does not do

We don't diagnose, treat disease, extract teeth or work below the gumline. Our care is maintenance between veterinary visits and never replaces a professional dental cleaning under anesthesia when your vet recommends one.

Results vary based on each pet's individual condition. Our anesthesia-free service focuses on preventive, above-the-gumline care and does not replace comprehensive veterinary dental treatment.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how our visits work, who they're for and how they fit with your vet's care.

Is anesthesia-free oral care safe?

Yes, when it is done by a trained technician on a pet that is calm and comfortable. We never force a pet. If your pet is too stressed, we stop and suggest another approach.

Does this replace a dental cleaning at the vet?

No. We work on visible tooth surfaces, above the gumline, as preventive maintenance. Only a veterinarian can examine below the gumline, take X-rays and treat dental disease. Our visits help keep things in good shape between those appointments.

How long does a visit take?

Usually 60 to 90 minutes, depending on your pet's size, temperament and how much buildup there is. We never rush a nervous pet.

What do I get after the visit?

A personalized smile report with before-and-after photos, notes on anything we noticed and simple home care recommendations. We also send a reminder when your pet is due again, usually in six months.

What if my pet is anxious or has never had a cleaning?

That is common. We go slowly, use breaks, calming music and gentle handling, and many of our clients started with pets that were scared of the vet.

Do you work with cats?

Yes. Cats have their own plan, with handling adapted to how cats like to be held.

Which areas do you serve?

We are mobile across Greater Dallas-Fort Worth, including Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Prosper, Celina and Denton. We also serve all surrounding communities, so ask us if your city is not listed.

Can you see more than one pet in the same visit?

Yes. Households with several pets can book them together, and we have family plans for that.
